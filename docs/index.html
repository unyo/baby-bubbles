Chloe's Bubbles

Support

A simple bubble-popping game for babies and toddlers. Tap, drag, shake, and tilt to pop bubbly animal friends, with sounds and gentle vibrations.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Turn off silent mode so your little one can hear the sounds.
  • To keep them inside the app, turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access), then triple-click the side button while the game is open.
  • Keep a firm grip during shaking.

More animals

Bears are free. A one-time purchase adds a cat, bunny, frog, pig, and chick. Tap Grown-ups: more animals on the start screen and answer the question to see it.

Got a new phone? Open the same grown-up screen and tap Restore purchase. You won't be charged again.
