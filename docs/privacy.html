Privacy Policy

Chloe's Bubbles · Effective November 1, 2026

Chloe's Bubbles is a bubble-popping game for babies and toddlers made by Cody Moniz. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use the internet to play, and it does not send anything about you or your child to us or to anyone else.

Device features the app uses

  • Motion sensors (shake and tilt) make the bubbles move. This happens only on the device. Motion data is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration and sound play feedback when bubbles pop. Nothing is recorded; the app does not use the microphone or camera.

In-app purchase

The optional "more animals" unlock is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The purchase screen is behind a grown-up question so children can't open it on their own.

Children's privacy

The app is made for young children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by not collecting any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no links out of the app, no chat, and no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Deleting your data

Because we don't collect any data, there is nothing for us to delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. A purchase you made stays with your Apple ID and can be restored from the app's grown-up screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. We will not start collecting personal information from children without first updating this policy and getting any consent the law requires.
